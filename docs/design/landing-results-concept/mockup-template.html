⚠️ Red Flags — Read Before Buying
  • ⚠

    Clutch replaced by an unlicensed mechanic

    No invoice, no warranty, no way to verify the work. Budget for it again.

  • ⚠

    Undocumented turbo upgrade

    Ask which shop, and ask for the tune log — a bad tune on this platform means a tired SR20, fast.

  • ⚠

    Surface rust noted, underside not shown

    Get it on a hoist before you commit. Cosmetic and structural rust look identical in a photo.

The Enthusiast Take

This is the platform everyone wants — SR20, RWD, six-speed, genuine Spec R. But "genuine" stops being genuine the moment nobody can prove what's been done to it. Walk in with a pre-purchase inspection booked, or walk away.
